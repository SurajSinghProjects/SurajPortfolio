Social Networks for Connections and Entertainment

Social networking sites that are focused explicitly on forming local and global communities of people are gaining immense momentum online. Mainly social media websites covering news and entertainment are pretty trendy. Hence, it makes sense to leverage the reach of such platforms to connect with prospective customers.

Story-based Social Network

Storytelling remains vital to establishing and maintaining a connection with people. Hence, it makes sense to leverage the power of storytelling via story-based social networking sites. You can invite bloggers to write engaging fiction, non-fiction, and biographical stories that could go viral and pull audiences, eventually attracting potential customers towards your business.

Community Social Network

Connecting like-minded people is what social media does best. People sharing interests like fashion or sports find each other on such social networking platforms. These portals allow people to form a community around their common interests where they feel comfortable sharing information, tips, and experiences.

Video/Photo Social Networking

The attention span of people is getting shorter and shorter every day. In this new era where everybody gets distracted every 7 seconds or so, videos and images are a lot more effective in grabbing people's attention than text. This is where we can help you create intricately engaging websites and applications that people find hard to take their eyes off.

Business Corporate/Professional Networking Websites

Social network website development provides an efficient system to help you network with various business entities while also allowing you to communicate professionally within the enterprise. We will help you create a fully equipped system with features like a profile page, a system for recommendations and endorsements, advanced search and stout privacy settings.

Microblogging

Microblogging is the new trend in town, and you should jump on the bandwagon without hesitation. With more and more people using the platform to broadcast important news, you can leverage the power of 140 characters to connect with your target audiences.
